7. FINDINGS AND RECOMMENDATIONS

Findings:

- One failed local login attempt was identified.
- Two successful local login sessions were identified.
- No evidence of brute-force activity or unauthorized access was identified within the reviewed authentication events.
- The UFW firewall was enabled and configured to restrict incoming connections.
- SSH access was restricted through a source-specific firewall rule and localhost NAT port forwarding.
- Authentication evidence was collected and a SHA-256 integrity hash was generated.

Recommendations:

- Continue monitoring authentication logs for suspicious activity.
- Investigate repeated failed login attempts.
- Maintain regular operating system and security updates.
- Implement SSH key-based authentication.
- Disable SSH password authentication after key-based access is verified.
- Regularly review firewall rules and listening network ports.
- Verify the integrity of collected forensic evidence.
- Implement centralized log monitoring using a SIEM platform.
